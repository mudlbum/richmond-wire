We will get things wrong. What matters is what happens next.

## Report an error

Email **{{CORRECTIONS_EMAIL}}** with the article's web address and, where possible, a source showing the correct information. You do not need to be the subject of the article to ask for a correction, and you do not need to explain why you care.

We aim to acknowledge within one working day.

## What we do about it

We use three categories, and we apply them consistently:

### Correction
A factual error — a wrong number, name, date, place, or attribution. We fix the text and add a dated correction note at the foot of the article stating what was wrong and what it now says. **We never quietly edit an error away.** The note stays permanently.

### Update
A developing story where the facts have moved on — a casualty figure revised by an authority, an outcome that has since been decided. We update the text and add a dated update note. This is not an admission of error; it is how developing stories work, and we would rather label it than let a stale figure sit there looking current.

### Clarification
The facts were right but the wording could reasonably mislead. We rewrite the passage and add a dated note explaining what changed.

If an article is so wrong that correcting it is not enough, we withdraw it. A withdrawn article's page stays live with the text replaced by an explanation of why it was withdrawn, so that inbound links do not lead to a dead end or, worse, a silently altered claim.

## What we will not do

- We will not remove an accurate, fairly reported article because its subject dislikes it.
- We will not correct something because it is politically inconvenient for someone.
- We will not delete a correction note once it is published.

## The most common case

Most of what we publish is a summary of other organisations' reporting. If a source we relied on has itself issued a correction, we correct too — but we will also say which source it came from, because you are entitled to weigh that.

## Right of reply

If you are named or directly described in an article and believe our characterisation is unfair, write to **{{CORRECTIONS_EMAIL}}**. Where a complaint is substantiated we will correct. Where it is a matter of disagreement rather than fact, we will consider adding your response to the article.

## Log of corrections

Corrections are recorded on the article they apply to. A consolidated log will appear on this page as entries accumulate.
