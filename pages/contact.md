## Corrections and factual disputes

If we have got something wrong — a number, a name, a date, a characterisation — this is the fastest way to fix it:

**{{CORRECTIONS_EMAIL}}**

Please include the article's web address and, if you can, a link to a source showing the correct information. We treat corrections as urgent. Our [corrections policy](/corrections/) explains what happens next and how we log changes in public.

## Copyright and rights holders

If you believe material on this site infringes your copyright, write to **{{EMAIL}}** with the words "Copyright" in the subject line. Include the specific web address, identification of the work, your contact details, and a statement of your good-faith belief that the use is not authorised. We investigate promptly and remove material where a claim is substantiated.

We quote briefly from published reporting for the purpose of news summary and comment, with attribution and a link back in every case. If you are a rights holder who would prefer we not summarise your reporting, tell us and we will stop.
