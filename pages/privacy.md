## The short version

We do not ask you to create an account, we do not run a newsletter, and we do not sell personal information. The site is funded by advertising, and advertising is where essentially all data collection on this site comes from. If you reject non-essential cookies, that collection is substantially reduced.

## What we collect

### Information you give us
Only what you send. If you email us, we hold your message and address for as long as needed to deal with it and to keep a record of any correction that resulted. We do not add you to any list.

### Information collected automatically
Our hosting provider records standard server request data — IP address, approximate location derived from it, browser and device type, the page requested, the referring page, and a timestamp. This is used to serve the site, keep it secure, and diagnose faults. It is not used to build a profile of you.

### Advertising and cookies
This site displays advertising served by third parties, including Google. Advertising involves cookies and similar technologies. Specifically:

- Third-party vendors, **including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites**.
- **Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.**
- **You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).** You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) or, in Europe, at [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/).
- Where Google's third-party ad partners are used, those vendors' own privacy policies govern their processing. A current list is published at [Google's advertising partners page](https://support.google.com/dfp_premium/answer/9012903).

Advertising cookies may be used to select ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure whether an ad worked, and detect fraud.

### What we do not do
We do not collect names, addresses, payment details or any special-category data. We do not run our own tracking pixels or fingerprinting. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

## Legal bases for processing

Where the UK/EU GDPR applies, we rely on:

- **Legitimate interests** — for serving the site, security, fault diagnosis and aggregate traffic measurement, balanced against your interests.
- **Consent** — for all non-essential cookies, including advertising cookies. You give consent through the banner shown on your first visit, and you can withdraw it at any time (see below).

## Consent, and how to change your mind

On your first visit you are asked whether to accept non-essential cookies. Advertising cookies are not set before you accept. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device; it never reaches us.

To change your answer, clear this site's data in your browser settings and reload the page — the banner will reappear. You can also block or delete cookies entirely in your browser; the site works normally without them.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, our advertising is operated in accordance with Google's EU user consent policy.

## Sharing

We share data with:

- **Our hosting and content-delivery providers**, who process server logs on our behalf in order to serve the site.
- **Advertising partners, including Google**, as described above, where you have consented.

We disclose information to public authorities only where we are legally required to, and we do not transfer personal data to anyone else for their own marketing purposes.

## International transfers

Our providers and advertising partners operate globally, so data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. Where personal data of EEA or UK residents is transferred, our providers rely on standard contractual clauses or an equivalent approved transfer mechanism.

## Retention

Server logs are retained for a short period — typically no more than 30 days — and then deleted or aggregated beyond identification. Email correspondence is retained as long as needed to handle the matter and to preserve the record of any correction. Cookie lifetimes are set by the parties that place them and are listed in our [cookie policy](/cookies/).

## Your choices {#your-choices}

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have data deleted;
- object to or restrict processing, including profiling for advertising;
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- **opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising** (California) — rejecting non-essential cookies on this site accomplishes this, and we honour Global Privacy Control signals where our providers transmit them.

To exercise any of these, email **{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}**. We do not charge for this and we do not treat you differently for asking.

Because we hold so little identifiable information, we may be unable to link a request to any specific record. In that case we will tell you rather than guess.
