Cookies are small files a website asks your browser to store. This page lists everything this site uses, what each thing is for, and how to say no. It is a companion to the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Strictly necessary

| What | Type | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `rin-consent` | Local storage | Remembers whether you accepted or rejected non-essential cookies, so you are not asked on every page | Until you clear site data |

This is set regardless of your choice, because it *is* your choice. It contains one word and is never transmitted to us.

## Advertising — only after you accept

These are set by Google and its advertising partners when you accept non-essential cookies. They are **not** set if you reject.

| What | Set by | Purpose | Typical lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ad selection and personalisation cookies | Google (`doubleclick.net`, `google.com`) | Choosing which ads to show, based on prior visits to this and other sites | Up to 2 years |
| Frequency capping | Google | Limiting how often you see the same advertisement | Days to months |
| Conversion and performance measurement | Google and partners | Measuring whether an ad led to an action | Up to 2 years |
| Fraud and invalid-traffic detection | Google | Detecting automated or fraudulent ad clicks | Session to months |

Exact names and lifetimes are set by those vendors and can change. Google publishes current details in its [advertising cookie documentation](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and a list of its ad-technology partners.

The advertising script itself is not loaded until you accept. Rejecting means the request to Google's ad servers is never made from this page, rather than being made and then ignored.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, consent for personalised advertising is collected through a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework, as Google requires of its publishers.

## What we do not use

We do not run our own analytics, tracking pixels, social-media buttons, embedded third-party comment systems, session replay, or browser fingerprinting. We do not use cookies to identify you personally or to build a profile of you for our own purposes.

## How to refuse or remove

- **On this site** — choose "Reject non-essential" on the banner. To change your answer later, clear this site's data in your browser and reload.
- **Personalised advertising generally** — [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads), [aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/), or [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/).
- **In your browser** — every major browser lets you block or delete cookies, and block third-party cookies specifically. This site works normally with cookies blocked; you will simply see non-personalised advertising or none at all.

Blocking cookies does not stop advertising from appearing. It stops it from being tailored to you.
